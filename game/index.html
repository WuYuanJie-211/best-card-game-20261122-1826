How to Duel

Both duelists refill to five cards and place two each into three lanes. You cannot cover all three — every placement is a bet on which lane you concede.

  • Bond +4 — two cards of the same suit in one lane.
  • Ladder +5 — two consecutive values, either order.
  • Twin +6 — two equal values. Highest combo only, never stacked.
  • Ember Wild — copies the other card's suit for a Bond, adds 0, never a Ladder or Twin.
  • Burn — once a round, discard a card to bank Heat equal to its value.
  • Surge — spend 4 Heat for one extra placement this round.
  • Kindled lane — one lane each round glows and deals double damage.

A lane won deals 1 Wick, +1 for your combo, +1 if Kindled. First to drop the other to 0 Wick takes the match. After round 8, higher Wick wins.

Read the Wickkeeper: he announces his strongest lane every round — and always plays his highest card there first. Gift him that lane and win the other two.
